## [0.7.0] — 2026-09-16

### Added

- **docs**: `docs/migration/0.7.md` covers every 0.6.x to 0.7.0 breaking and
  action-required change, written from the actual removal diff (#961).
- **cli**: `support --advise` evaluates parsed copybook constructs under the
  requested options and reports a closed verdict with remediation guidance;
  machine output follows the versioned beta contract
  `schemas/advise-result.json` (`0.7.0-beta.1`), never human prose (#954).
- **codec**: VB/BDW (variable-blocked) record framing works end to end across
  decode, encode, and verify paths (#953).
- **ledger**: scenario ledger contract with seed fixtures and verifier backs
  advisory assessments and corpus evidence with stable scenario IDs (#953).

### Changed

- **workspace**: MSRV raised from Rust 1.95 to Rust 1.98 workspace-wide
  (#959).

### Removed

- **facade**: the eight deprecated 0.6 compatibility aliases
  (`copybook::{codepage, contracts, determinism, fixed, options, overpunch,
  rdw, record_io}`) now resolve only through their canonical owner paths
  (#960).
- **workspace**: the 0.6 compatibility shims in `copybook-codepage`,
  `copybook-lexer`, and `copybook-dialect` are removed; the crates remain
  published under their names as empty shells (#960).

### Security

- **deps**: rustls raised to 0.23.45 for RUSTSEC-2026-0285 (#956).
